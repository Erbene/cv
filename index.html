01 · Distributed systems Target Solutions · 2014–2018

BiPTT — exclusive floor control under latency constraints

Owned the product end to end as technical lead and primary architect: roadmap, distributed architecture, AWS operations, and customer support. Designed exclusive channel floor control—one speaker at a time, speakers also excluded from private calls—with up to ~1,000 contenders per channel. Serialized lock acquisition through a per-channel GenServer, reclaimed stale locks via a 2-second silence timeout on the audio stream, and applied consistency per operation: forced full cross-node replication where strict exclusion was required.

Designed Protocol Buffers/TCP signaling and UDP audio streaming against a sub-20ms requirement; partitioned Mnesia channel state by channel ID. User state was managed separately in Elixir; authentication was a separate system. A tailored São Paulo metro deployment for ~200 operational users cost about one-fifth of a conventional Motorola quote, which would have required several antennas and repeaters along the lines, saving ~R$2 million (~US$0.8 million at the time). Led one junior engineer and two interns.
